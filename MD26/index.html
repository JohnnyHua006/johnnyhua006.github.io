A Letter from Gucci

Dearest Mom,

I want you to know I have thought about this letter very carefully, the way I think about which spot on the couch deserves my company.

You are my person. Yes, Dad is fine. He is tall and useful. But you are the one who knows exactly how I like the blanket arranged, and you never act like that is a strange thing to know.

Today I will let you hold me longer than usual. I will not pretend I have somewhere to be. This is my gift.

Also: please do not tell Chewie I wrote a longer letter. He will get competitive about it.

All my love (and most of my patience),

Gucci

A Letter from Chewie

HI MOM!!

It is me. CHEWIE. Did you know it is Mother's Day? I did not until five minutes ago and now I am very excited and may need to do a lap around the house.

You are the best mom. You let me sniff every bush on the walk. You give me the crusts. You said "good boy" forty-seven times yesterday and yes I was counting.

I love you with my whole tail. That is a LOT of love because my tail is enormous and very expressive.

I made you a card. The card is me. I am the card.

Your favorite child,

Chewie
